# Panes: the app performs the song

The whole film is one Dormouse window. Nobody appears in it: the panes split, tile, ring and minimize on the words and the beat. Under the window a prompt line carries the lyric, each word lighting as it is sung.

Times are film seconds. Scene ids are the files in `compositions/`.

## intro (0.0–13.5)

"Do-do-Dormouse"
**[One shell and its cursor. The wall stutters into four columns that spell `Do-` `do-` `Dor` `mouse`, one pane per eighth note.]**

"Dormouse"
**[They fold to two panes: `Dor`, `mouse`. Both minimize to doors on the baseboard.]**

(instrumental)
**[A fresh shell. Someone types `> dormouse` at full height, the cursor blinking on the beat.]**

"Do-do-Dormouse, Dormouse, Dormouse"
**[Every word is another pane and the wall re-tiles to fit: 4, then 6, then 8. The words give way to work as each pane starts printing.]**

"Dormouse, Dormouse"
**[Four hits. Two panes drop to the baseboard on each, and the ones still up re-tile to fill the wall, until it is empty.]**

## verse1 (13.5–18.8)

"Agent to the left, another agent to the right"
**[`claude` reveals from the left edge and starts work. `codex` reveals from the right.]**

"Dev server at the bottom, information packed-in tight"
**[The agents make room and `npm run dev` comes up from below. Then every pane splits: eleven panes, all printing, and they squeeze together on "tight".]**

## pre (18.8–24.1)

"Alerts pop up when I need to know"
**[A pane rings and takes a TODO, and a notice pops up beside it. Twice more, on "pop" and "up", with the doors ringing too. On "need to know" the focus ring walks to each one and its TODO resolves.]**

"C-L-I automation to build my own flow"
**[Everything else closes and one shell takes the left of the wall. `dor split -- claude` is typed, then `dor ensure -- npm run dev` and `dor agent-browser open :5173` land on "build" and "own". Each command makes a Surface on the right: an agent, a dev server, a browser.]**

## chorus1 (24.1–34.8)

"Dormouse, Dormouse, multitask more"
**[One pane becomes 2, 4, 8, 16: a doubling on each "Dormouse", on "multitask" and on "more". The two words land across the wall.]**

"Dormouse, Dormouse, D-O-R spells dor"
**[Sixteen fold to four, then to one shell. It types `$ d`, `o`, `r`, a letter each. On "dor" the word lights and the `dor` verbs print under it.]**

"Dormouse, Dormouse, VS Code or standalone"
**[Two more panes. On "VS Code" the editor's frame slides in from every edge and the wall becomes its panel. On "standalone" the frame leaves and the same wall is its own window on the desktop.]**

"Dormouse, Dormouse, from my laptop to my phone"
**[Full screen, a fourth pane. Then the camera backs off: the window is on a laptop, packets cross, and a phone comes up beside it running the same agent.]**

## verse2 (34.8–45.8)

"Browser repping in the front, terminal party in the back"
**[A browser pane with a page in front. Behind it, offset, its terminal, the header running through the ANSI colors on every sixteenth while rainbow output prints.]**

"A Dor Tool is a mullet, I can build with any stack"
**[One ring wraps both, labelled `tool: mullet`, because they are one Surface. The Tool makes room and six more Tools drop into a stack beside it, one per word: vite, rails, django, phoenix, cargo, storybook.]**

"Vibe-coded in an evening, but still easy to maintain"
**[Left, an agent builds it while a clock runs from 6:00 PM to 9:00 PM. Right, `dormouse.yml` fills in: five lines.]**

"Mouse and keys at my desk, and just my thumbs on the train"
**[A pointer clicks a pane and the focus moves. The `-` key splits it top and bottom, the `|` key left and right. Then the window shrinks into a phone, lights start passing behind it, and two thumbs tap.]**

## break (45.8–53.5)

"What app is that?" / "Oh I'm vibescrolling, girl" / "Dawg, vibescrolling?" / "I'm not a dog, I'm a"
**[The phone on the train, its feed flicked up on every beat. Two panes, `her` and `him`, one per voice, each word arriving as it is said.]**

(instrumental bar)
**[The window answers for him: eight panes, one per eighth note, spell D-O-R-M-O-U-S-E.]**

## chorus2 (53.5–64.1)

The same four lines and the same moves as chorus 1, in a different editor theme for each line: light, Monokai, Solarized, then back to dark.

## end (64.1–74.0)

"Do-Dormouse.sh, baby"
**[The logo lands. `dormouse.sh` is typed beside it, and "Multitasking terminal for mice" comes up under it on "baby".]**

"Dormouse, Dormouse, Dormouse, Dormouse"
**[Four panes take the corners, one per word: in VS Code, standalone, on your phone, `$ dor`. The logo kicks on each. Fade on the last note.]**
