# Dormouse Multitask: "type"

A kinetic-typography lyric video. The lyrics are the picture: huge Archivo Black-weight type on flat color fields that cut on the bar, with terminal glyphs (`>`, `$`, `_`, a cursor block, split-pane rectangles) as the only graphics. The real icon appears once, on the end card.

Palette: ink `#1f1b18`, the icon's charcoal `#43423d`, a louder version of the icon's orange `#e8834a`, cream `#f3eadb`, cobalt `#2b45d9`, and lime `#d9ff3d` kept for alerts and highlights. Faces: Archivo variable (weight 900, width 62–125%, the width axis is animated), JetBrains Mono for anything a terminal would say, Instrument Serif italic for "her" and for the business half of the mullet.

## Intro (bars 0–4)

- Charcoal. An orange `>` prompt and a cream cursor block keep the beat.
- "Do-do-Dormouse" stutters as four eighth notes: DO (cream, left), DO (orange, right), DOR, then the full DORMOUSE with an orange underscore cursor.
- "Dormouse": cut to orange, `> dormouse` typed above, DORMOUSE in ink drifting left.
- Bar 2: the frame tiles into four panes (orange, charcoal, cobalt, ink) on the beat; the second stutter puts one syllable in each pane: DO / DO / DOR / MOUSE.
- "Dormouse, Dormouse": a condensed DORMOUSE drops into the top half (ink on orange), then another rises into the bottom half (cobalt on cream).
- Bar 4: charcoal; DORMOUSE as a cream outline, then filled orange over it with a shake.

## Verse 1 (bars 5–6)

- A lone cursor on cream. "Agent to the left": the left half wipes in as an orange pane, AGENT / to the / LEFT,.
- "another agent to the right": a cobalt pane wipes in from the right, ANOTHER AGENT / to the / RIGHT.
- "Dev server at the bottom": both panes shrink to make room for a charcoal pane rising from below, `$ dev server` in mono with a cursor, and BOTTOM, slamming up.
- "information packed-in tight": a lime band crosses all three panes; INFORMATION starts wide and loosely tracked and is squeezed to the narrowest width and negative tracking; "packed-in" (mono) and TIGHT (orange) land under it as the panes close in.

## Pre-chorus (bars 7–8)

- Charcoal. ALERTS slides in, POP pops in lime, UP jumps up from below; six lime alert badges with `!` pop around them every eighth note with expanding rings.
- "when I need to know" types into a cream notification toast.
- Cobalt. "C-L-I": three terminal cells, each flashing orange as its letter lands. `$ automation` types out with a lime block caret.
- "to build my own" in mono; FLOW in wide orange, each letter riding a sine wave as the word drifts right.

## Chorus 1 (bars 9–12)

- c1a: DORMOUSE, slams cream on charcoal, then ink on orange (shake on both). "multitask": four panes (`> 1`–`> 4`) roll in a sixteenth apart, each with MULTITASK. "more": a lime field opens from a band and MORE lands, extra-wide.
- c1b: cobalt. DORMOUSE runs up the left edge (cream) and down the right edge (orange). "D-O-R": three ink cells light D, O, R in the middle, and the D, O and R of both edge columns turn lime with them. "spells", then `$ dor` in lime with a blinking cursor.
- c1c: ink. Two DORMOUSE, lines fly in from opposite sides (orange solid, cream outline). "VS Code" fills a cobalt top band, `or` in lime mono on the seam, STANDALONE fills an orange bottom band.
- c1d: cream. Two DORMOUSE, rows pass each other. Charcoal: a 16:9 frame of type, "from my" / LAPTOP; on "to my phone" the frame morphs into a phone-shaped column and PHONE stacks down it letter by letter in lime.

## Verse 2 (bars 13–16)

- v2a: cobalt. A cream browser window (three dots, an address pill) in front: BROWSER / repping in the / FRONT,. A charcoal terminal card waits behind. On "terminal" the two trade places: TERMINAL in orange, "party" pops in lime mono, "in the", BACK.
- v2b: ink. A DOR TOOL / is a / then the mullet: "Mul" in a serif on a cream panel (business in front) and "let," in heavy mono on an orange panel (party in the back), one word. "I can build with any", and STACK lands as five colored blocks dropped from the bottom up, so it reads top to bottom.
- v2c: orange with a lime sun. VIBE-CODED, skewed and wobbling on the beat; "in an"; on EVENING, the field cuts to cobalt and the sun sinks. Then a strict twelve-column grid on ink: "but still" / EASY in lime / "to" / MAINTAIN, everything square on the grid.
- v2d: cream left half: MOUSE / and / K-E-Y-S as keycaps / at my / DESK, on a desk line. A cobalt right half wipes in: "and just my" / THUMBS / "on the", and a train (ink body, cream windows) pulls in and stops on TRAIN.

## Break (bars 17–19)

- Two typographic voices: hers in Instrument Serif italic, cream on a cobalt left half; his in JetBrains Mono, ink and cream on an orange right half. Whoever speaks gets the wider half; the other voice's words are clipped by the narrowing pane.
- Her: "What app / is that?" Him: "Oh I'm / vibescrolling, / girl" over a feed of "vibescrolling" scrolling up in tone-on-tone orange.
- Her: "Dawg," huge, "vibescrolling?" in lime. Him: "I'm not a dog," and on "dog" an orange bar strikes through her "Dawg,"; then "I'm a" in large cream.
- Bar 19, the wind-up: his half takes the whole frame, three dots after "I'm a" land on the beats, a mono loading bar fills to 100% in accelerating steps, and an ink cursor block grows from the center to swallow the frame.

## Chorus 2 (bars 20–23)

Chorus 1, turned up.

- c2a: the letters of DORMOUSE, fly in from every direction, assemble on a cream flash, and blow apart again. Then a wall of six rows of DORMOUSE, (cream, orange, outline, lime) sliding in alternate directions. "multitask": sixteen panes in a diagonal cascade. "more": lime opens as a circle, MORE lands with four outline echoes zooming outward.
- c2b: DORMOUSE, tilted -8° on cobalt, then +8° on orange. "D-O-R": each letter gets the whole frame on its own field (lime, cobalt, orange). "spells", then `dor` huge in lime mono with an orange cursor, with a shake.
- c2c: cream. One DORMOUSE, stretches from the narrowest width to the widest; a second, in orange, squeezes from wide and loose to narrow. Then a diagonal split: VS CODE on a cobalt triangle, STANDALONE on an orange one, `or` in a lime circle on the seam.
- c2d: DORMOUSE, strobes cream/ink on the beat. Then the laptop frame and the phone side by side: "from my" LAPTOP, and on "to" the orange cursor block leaps from the laptop into the phone, which floods cobalt as PHONE stacks down it.

## Tag (bar 24)

- Cream, an ink cursor block waiting. "Do-" in orange, "Dor" in ink, and an ink address bar rises: `dor` in lime, then `mouse.sh` types in cream, with a blinking orange cursor: `dormouse.sh`. "baby" spins in as an orange serif italic signature.

## Outro (bars 25–27)

- Each of the last four "Dormouse"s slides in a full-width stripe from alternating sides, each in one of the film's voices: condensed Archivo (orange), mono (cobalt), serif italic (cream), outline (lime), numbered `> 1`–`> 4` like panes.
- Just after the fourth, the stripes shoot off alternately to reveal the end card on ink: the real Dormouse icon, DORMOUSE in cream, and `dormouse.sh` typing in orange with a blinking cursor, held to the end.
