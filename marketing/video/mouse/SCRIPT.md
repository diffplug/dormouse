# "mouse": the treatment

A character cartoon. One flat, inked, paper-cutout world, and one star: the dormouse from the logo, rigged so it can sing, bop and act. The lyric is a sing-along line in a dark band along the bottom, written as the terminal's prompt, and each word lights as it is sung.

Times are film seconds. Every scene starts on a bar, except the break, which waits for "train" to finish.

## intro (0.0–13.5, bars 0–4)

**The logo's tile sits alone on the floor, squashing on the beat, its cursor blinking on the off-beat.** "Do-do-Dormouse": ears come up behind it in two stutters, then the whole head, singing. "Dormouse": it leans out round the left side. In the quiet bar it looks over the top, left and right. "Do-do-Dormouse": round the right side, in stutters. "Dormouse, Dormouse": up over the top, then a jump onto the tile as the camera pulls back. "Dormouse, Dormouse": "Dor" lands on its left and "mouse" on its right.

## verse1 (13.5–18.8, bars 5–6)

**The mouse conducts.** "Agent to the left": a hand goes out and a robot rolls in under its own pane. "another agent to the right": the baton goes out and a second one rolls in. "Dev server at the bottom": a rack comes up through the floor under the mouse's feet and lifts it. "information packed-in tight": panes pop into every gap, and on "tight" the whole room squeezes.

## pre (18.8–24.1, bars 7–8)

**Same room.** "Alerts pop up": a bell over the left agent, the right agent, and a pane, and the mouse looks at each. "when I need to know": one wave of the baton and each bell becomes a tick. "C-L-I automation": a prompt opens over the top row and the mouse types `dor split -- claude` and `dor await --until quiet`. "to build my own flow": wires run from the prompt to each agent and along the floor to the server, and on "flow" everything lights.

## juggle (24.1–26.8, bar 9)

**Yellow.** The mouse dances, big, in the middle. A wheel of panes turns behind it, gaining a pane on every word and speeding up on "multitask more".

## spell (26.8–29.5, bar 10)

**Teal.** Three cards drop in face down. "D-O-R": the pointer raps each and it turns over. "spells": they close up. "dor": they become a terminal that says `$ dor_`.

## hosts (29.5–32.1, bar 11)

**Pink.** An editor window with Dormouse in its bottom panel, and Dormouse as its own window. "VS Code": the mouse jumps onto the first. "or standalone": a long jump onto the second.

## devices (32.1–34.8, bar 12)

**Blue.** The mouse is on a laptop's screen, waving. "from my laptop": it points across. "to my phone": it ducks out, a small mouse head arcs over, the phone wakes, and a smaller mouse pops up inside with its panes stacked.

## mullet (34.8–37.5, bar 13)

**The joke the verse is built on.** "Browser repping in the front": a browser window slides in and a neat fringe grows. "terminal party in the back": the mouse turns its back, and there is a lot more hair there, with terminal text in it. The room goes dark, lights pulse, and terminals bounce around it.

## tool (37.5–42.8, bars 14–15)

**The mouse keeps the mullet from here until the last scene.** "A Dor Tool is a mullet": a window that is half browser and half terminal, and a wig drops onto it. "I can build with any stack": blocks named Go, Python, Rust and React drop into a pile, and the mouse hops on top. "Vibe-coded in an evening": the sky turns, the sun sets, the moon and stars come out, and the mouse lies back with its eyes shut. "but still easy to maintain": it opens its eyes and points, and the Tool gets a tick.

## desk (42.8–45.8, bar 16)

**Split screen.** "Mouse and keys at my desk": the mouse behind a desk, and the computer mouse and the keyboard each jump as they are named. "and just my thumbs on the train": a train car slides in over the right half, with the same mouse on a bench, thumbs on a phone.

## break (45.8–53.5, bars 17–19)

**The train, wide. A second mouse with a bow sits beside ours.** Speech bubbles fill in word by word. Her: "What app is that?", leaning over. Him, not looking up: "Oh I'm vibescrolling, girl", with a wink on "girl". Her, sitting back: "Dawg, vibescrolling?" Him, eyebrows down, phone dropped: "I'm not a dog, I'm a". Then the bar with no words: the camera closes on his face in four steps, one per beat, the car turns yellow, he fills his lungs, and his mouth opens.

## party (53.5–64.1, bars 20–23)

**"DORMOUSE!"** fills the screen over a sunburst, with him under it, arms up. It lifts away on "multitask" to show the whole car dancing: him, her, a grey mouse and two robots, the straps swinging, the wall a new color each line. Over their heads: signs for "multitask" and "more!", a D, an O and an R that close up into DOR, signs for "VS Code" and "standalone", then a laptop and a phone with panes flying from one to the other.

## end (64.1–74.0, bars 24–27)

**Close on his face, pink.** A terminal types `$ dormouse.sh`. "baby": he winks, a star glints, and the wig flies off. On bar 25 the close-up slides away to the end card: the logo's tile, and the mouse jumps in beside it on the first "Dormouse", puts a hand on the prompt on the second, `dormouse.sh` lands on the third, and "Multitasking terminal for mice" on the fourth. It keeps bopping and blinks twice before the end.
